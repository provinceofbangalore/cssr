Hi there!
We're so glad you took the time to visit our website!
This page is a point of contact especially for young men discerning a call to priesthood; men and women discerning a call to religous life; or men and women who would like to become lay associates for the Redemptorist Congregation within India.
The official name of the Redemptorists is the Congregation of the Most Holy Redeemer. It was founded on 9th November 1732 by St. Alphonsus Liguori for the purpose of evangelizing the poor and most abandoned. Today the Congregation has grown into a worldwide family with over 5000 members doing missionary work in over 80 countries! Get to know this great big family of Redemptorist Fathers, Brothers, Sisters and lay missionaries better by visiting the Redemptorist community nearest to you.
If you reside in India and you're seriously considering a vocation to priesthood or the religious life in our Congregation please email us to "https://web.archive.org/web/20260713001513/mailto:ryvmblrcssr@gmail.com" or drop a message to us on any of our social media pages. We'll be glad to guide you forward in your discernment. 
Don't be afraid to follow the call of God wherever it may lead you! It always leads to greater joy and purpose for your life.
