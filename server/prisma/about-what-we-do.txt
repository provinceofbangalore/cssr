A. PARISH MISSIONS



Redemptorists are known for their parish missions. Especially during the season of Advent and Lent popular parish missions are preached at various parishes throughout India in English and many other Indian languages and dialects.
    The main purpose of the missions is the renewal of the entire parish with a call to repentance and conversion. There was a time when the Redemptorists were called Redem-terrorists, for their terrorizing sermons on death and hell fire. But today the focus of the preaching is on the unconditional love of God and the persons response to this love, which necessarily lead to change of heart.

As the Redemptorists are given the privilege and responsibility to popularize the devotion to the Mother of Perpetual Succour, they conduct and preach for special novenas at various parishes both Redemptorist and others. Redemptorists are also invited for preaching for novenas in preparation for Parish feasts and other occasions. 

C. PREACHING RETREATS


Retreats to various groups such as priests, religious, laity, students, teachers, seminarians and the like are preached by the Redemptorists all through the year. At Nava Spoorthi Kendra, a Redemptorist Retreat cum Pastoral centre at Bangalore, retreats and other programmes for different groups are conducted regularly.


d. PARISH MINISTRY

Serving the faithful in the parishes form a considerable part of their Apostolate. There are a number of parishes that are entrusted for the care of the Redemptorists. A sizable number of Redemptorists are engaged in this ministry all over the province.

E. TEACHING APOSTOLATE


A number of Redemptorists are involved in the apostolate of teaching. The Bangalore province has its own Theologate called St. Alphonsus College affiliated to St. Peters Pontifical Institute of Theology, Bangalore.  Students from the other Redemptorists units in India (Liguori Province and the Region of Mumbai) and some from other seminaries study here along with those of the Province of Bangalore. Besides, teaching at our own college, our professors teach Scripture, Moral Theology, Dogmatic Theology and Philosophy also at other seminaries and institutes


F. JUSTICE AND PEACE MINISTRY


The Redemptorists faithful to their Constitutions (no. 5) show preference for situations where there is pastoral need. Besides, their mandate "to evangelize the poor is directed to the liberation and salvation of the whole human person." They have "the duty of preaching the Gospel explicitly and of showing solidarity with the poor by promoting their fundamental rights to justice and freedom. Those for whom the Redemptorists work are Domestic workers, Dalits, Fish workers, Weavers, Migrants etc. Issues of Justice and Peace also form significant part of their preaching ministry.
